## IF YOU GET STUCK

**Problem:** No sales by Day 25
- **Fix:** Lower price, add bonus, or switch to free + paid upsell

**Problem:** Don't know what to build
- **Fix:** Look at your CSV for "training," "guide," "template" — pick one

**Problem:** Tech too hard
- **Fix:** Use Gumroad/Payhip only — no custom app needed

**Problem:** No audience
- **Fix:** Post 3x daily on 1 platform for 30 days (TikTok recommended)
